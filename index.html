Privacy Policy for Pixel Patch

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Pixel Patch is a single-player game. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, and it does not require an internet connection to play.

What the app does

  • All game progress (coins, crops, achievements, settings) is saved only on your device, using standard local app storage. It is never uploaded anywhere.
  • The app makes no network requests of any kind — no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no third-party SDKs.
  • There is no account creation, sign-in, or linking to any external service.
  • The app requests no device permissions (no camera, location, contacts, microphone, etc.).
  • There are no in-app purchases and no real-money transactions of any kind.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it is safe for players of any age, including children under 13. Pixel Patch is fully compliant with COPPA and similar children's privacy regulations by virtue of collecting nothing at all.

Changes to this policy

If this app ever adds a feature that changes what is described above (for example, optional cloud save or analytics), this policy will be updated first, and the update will clearly describe what changed and why.
